How to Make a Wrapped Bouquet

Step 1: Choose and Arrange Your Flowers

Choose what flowers you plan to wrap.

I would recommend getting your flowers from a local flower shop if you can. If that isn't possible, grocery stores or places like Walmart usually have them as well, though they aren't generally as cared for as those from a florist and many not last as long.

For this example, I have chosen a rose, a disbud, a daisy stem, and some baby's breath.

However, you can choose whatever type and as many flowers as you'd like. Keep in mind though, that the more flowers there are, the trickier it is to get them to stay in place during the wrapping process.

Lay them flat on your work surface and arrange them in your desired formation. I recommend putting more flowers on the left side of the arrangement, as the right side may be somewhat obscured by the wrapping paper later on.


Step 2: Trim the Ends

Next, you'll trim the ends of the stems.

Some florists use knives to do this, but I use clippers, and I would recommend the same for someone who is inexperienced. You can get clippers like mine at a hardware store. Don't use scissors, as they aren't sharp enough and can cause the stem to be pinched shut.

Always cut the ends of the stems at an angle, as it allows the flowers to suck up water more efficiently.

Your stems should all be roughly the same length, as shorter pieces won't reach the foam later.





Step 3: Wrap the Ends in Foam

After you've trimmed your ends, you'll want to move quickly for this next part. If the ends of the stems are exposed to air for too long, a blockage will form in the stem, making it harder for the flowers to drink water.

There are two ways you can do this. First, you can use a water tube. If that is the case, you fill the water tube, put the lid on, and stick your flowers in. You can then skip to Step 6.

The second way is to use foam. The foam I have comes in triangle sheets, and the flowers should be placed with the longest side of the triangle facing the heads of your flowers. You will then fold the bottom tip over the top of the stems. Then, you will fold one of the sides over, and do the same for the other side.

Finally, you will secure it with a rubber band.

Step 4: Soak Foam in Water

You will then place the foam in a container of water; I am using a pitcher.

Optionally, you can put some preservative in the water if you would like.

If you got your flowers from Walmart or a grocery store, they usually come with a little packet of preservative. If you are getting your flowers from a flower shop, they most likely have some on hand as well.

Make sure the foam is submerged completely. You will then let it sit for 2-3 minutes to soak up water for the stems.





Step 5: Wrap the Foam

Once you've let the foam sit in water for a few minutes, you will have to cover it to prevent water from leaking everywhere.

I usually wrap the foam in cling film, and then put a small plastic bag over the top. You don't necessarily have to use the cling film, I just like to have the extra layer of protection.

I use another rubber band to secure both the bag and the cling film in place. If the plastic bag is on the longer side, you can fold it over so that it doesn't show when the paper goes on.

Step 6: Wrap the Bouquet in Paper

You will then place the bouquet on top of your wrapping paper diagonally.

You will then fold the edge nearest to the foam over the top of it, in a similar fashion to how the foam was folded over the stems. Then, will fold the corner on the left side over the flowers. You will then bend the very tip back over so that the flowers are visible.

Then, you will take the right side, fold it over the flowers, and tuck the tip underneath.

Step 7: Tie the Bow

Lastly, you will need a ribbon of your choice. You can find ribbon at a craft store such as Joann or Michaels.

You will use this ribbon to tie the paper in place with a knot. Then, tie a bow and trim the tails of the ribbon how you see fit. You can cut them straight, at an angle, in a V-shape. It's up to you.












You now have a finished wrapped bouquet!
