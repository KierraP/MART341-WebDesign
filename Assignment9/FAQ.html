Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use whatever flowers I want?

Yes, you can use whatever flowers you want, and as many as you want.


Do I have to put the flowers in water?

You can wrap them without using a water tube or foam, but just know that the flowers will not last nearly as long without water supply.


Do I have to use preservative in the water?

It's not absolutely essential, but it will make the flowers last longer.
