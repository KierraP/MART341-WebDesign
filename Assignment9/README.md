# How to Make a Wrapped Bouquet
1.	Choose and arrange your flowers.
2.	Trim the ends at an angle.
3.	Wrap the ends in foam.
4.	Soak the foam in water.
5.	Wrap the foam.
6.	Wrap the bouquet in paper.
7.	Tie a bow.

# Materials
- Flowers
- Clippers
- Wrapping Paper
- Water Tube/Hydration Foam
- Cling Film
- Plastic Bag
- Ribbon
