Wrapped Bouquet Materials

What you'll need:

    Flowers - Can be obtained at a flower shop, grocery store, or your own garden!
    Clippers - Can be bought at a hardware or department store. The kind I use can be found here.
    Wrapping Paper - You specifically want wax floral paper for this. There are a lot of different options for where to get different colors and patterns. Here's some you can get on Amazon.
    Water Tube or Foam - You can either choose to use a Water Tube or Hydration Foam. You don't need both.
    Cling Film - You can get this at a grocery store. I use this. You only need this if you chose to use the foam.
    Small Plastic Bag - If you use the foam I linked above, it comes with its own bags. You can also get your own here. If you choose the water tube, you don't need this.
    Ribbon - Craft stores like Joann and Michaels have lots of options for ribbon.
